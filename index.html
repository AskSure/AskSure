AskSure

Privacy Policy & Support

Effective date: 19 October 2026

In short

AskSure helps you assess messages for possible scams. It does not require an account, does not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. Message text is sent for analysis only after you explicitly agree.

Who we are

AskSure is provided by Digital Pulse d.o.o. (“we”, “us”, or “our”). If you have a privacy question, a request about your data, or need support, email hello@digitalpulsedoo.com.

Information the app handles

  • Message text and links. You may type or paste text, or share text or a link into AskSure. This content can include personal information if you choose to provide it.
  • Images you select or share. Text is extracted from an image on your device using on-device OCR. The image itself is not sent for analysis.
  • Results and settings stored on your device. If history is enabled, AskSure stores completed checks locally. It also stores your language and consent preferences locally.

AskSure does not ask you to create an account and does not attach an account identifier or device identifier to an analysis request.

How analysis works

Before the first analysis, AskSure asks for your consent. Once you agree, the message text you choose to check is sent over the network to TypeSafe’s service at typesafe.pro to provide the scam-risk assessment. We send text, not the source image, local history, or a device identifier. If you do not consent, AskSure does not send text for analysis.

TypeSafe processes submitted text as a service provider for the analysis feature. Its handling of information is subject to its own terms and privacy practices. Do not submit information that you would not want a third-party analysis provider to process.

Local storage and your choices

  • Completed-check history is stored locally on your device for up to 30 days by default. You can turn off future history, delete individual records, or clear all history in the app’s settings.
  • An unfinished text draft and items shared into the app expire after up to 24 hours. You can erase the draft or clear locally stored data in the app.
  • Shared text and images are held in protected local storage until they are imported, discarded, or expire. They are not used to start an analysis automatically.
  • AskSure does not use CloudKit backup for its check history or shared-import storage.

No advertising or tracking

AskSure does not sell personal information, use advertising SDKs, use analytics SDKs, or track you across apps, websites, or services for advertising purposes.

Security

Locally stored sensitive content is protected by iOS data-protection controls and excluded from device backup where supported. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please use care when deciding what to submit for analysis.

Your rights and questions

You can control most AskSure data directly in the app by deleting local history, drafts, and shared items. For questions, concerns, or applicable privacy-rights requests, contact us at hello@digitalpulsedoo.com. We may update this policy when the app or its processing changes; the effective date above will be updated with the new version.

Support

For app support, email hello@digitalpulsedoo.com and include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Please do not include passwords, verification codes, payment details, or other sensitive content in support requests.
